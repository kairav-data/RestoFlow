# Customer Experience

Customer experience is mobile-first.

Primary journey:

QR
→ Restaurant
→ Discover
→ Choose
→ Order
→ Track
→ Pay
→ Review

Reduce friction.

Avoid unnecessary forms.

Use large touch targets.

Food discovery should be visual.

AI recommendations should explain why an item was recommended.

Always provide:

- price
- rating
- dietary information
- spice level
- availability

Do not overwhelm customers with too many choices.

Prioritize:

View Menu
Help Me Choose
Popular Now
Order Now
Pay Bill
Review
